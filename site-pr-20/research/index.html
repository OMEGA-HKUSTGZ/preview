Multimodal Perception

Representing the physical world

We study visual perception, 3D geometry, multimodal fusion and scene representations, combining visual, geometric and other sensory information to understand objects and their surroundings.

UniT recovers metric-scale geometry from any number of views, shown here reconstructing an indoor scene at HKUST(GZ). The project page includes more reconstructions, results and a live demo.
