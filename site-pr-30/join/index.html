RESEARCH ASSISTANT OPPORTUNITIES

Looking for RAs

We are looking for research assistants at OMEGA Lab, HKUST(GZ). Choose the track that matches your goals: research experience before postgraduate study, or a research support role open to all academic disciplines.

01

RA — Prospective students

For students and graduates considering a future MPhil or PhD who would like to gain research experience with OMEGA. Work with us on questions in multimodal understanding, intent and motion prediction, world modeling, or agents that reason and act. Tell us what you would like to investigate and share any relevant projects, code, or research experience.

Include your CV, a brief research interest statement, a transcript if available, and your intended start date, duration and weekly availability. An RA appointment does not guarantee postgraduate admission; degree applications follow the university process.

Email about RA - Prospective students
02

RA — Research support

For applicants seeking a research assistant role supporting the day-to-day work of the lab. Applicants from all academic disciplines are welcome; a future postgraduate application is not required. Depending on your strengths and project needs, work may include literature and information organization, data collection and annotation, experiment support, project coordination, documentation, or research communication.

We value reliability, attention to detail, clear communication, organization and willingness to learn. Programming is useful for some tasks but is not a general requirement for this track. Send your CV, a short description of relevant skills or experience, and your intended start date, duration and weekly availability.

Email about RA - Research support

BEFORE YOU WRITE

Common questions

Does an email count as a university application?

Email is a way to introduce yourself and discuss research fit. Degree applicants must also complete the official HKUST(GZ) application. Admission decisions, requirements and deadlines follow the university process.

I am already at HKUST(GZ). How can I get involved?

Email Xinhu with your current programme, research interests and relevant experience. We can discuss possible projects and how they fit your programme's supervision and study requirements.

Which research areas should I mention?

Start with a question that interests you: understanding multimodal observations, modeling intent and motion, learning world representations, or building agents that reason and act. A concrete project or question helps start the conversation.

Can I apply for a research support RA role from a non-technical major?

Yes. The research support track welcomes applicants from all academic disciplines, including humanities, social sciences, business and design. Describe the practical skills you can contribute. You do not need to plan to pursue an MPhil or PhD.

What are the RA appointment terms?

Responsibilities, remuneration, working hours, appointment duration and work location are discussed for each role and confirmed through the university appointment process. The postgraduate studentship figures above apply to eligible degree students, not RA salaries.
