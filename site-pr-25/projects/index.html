LightVLN

Preprint

First public: Oct 4, 2026 · Lab-led research

First author: Yiming Zhao

LightVLN is a lightweight aerial vision-and-language navigation framework that combines a compact language backbone, single-token historical memory, and history- and instruction-conditioned local aggregation to reduce inference costs while maintaining competitive navigation performance and enabling real-time closed-loop onboard-compute HIL evaluation on Jetson Orin NX. In this HIL setup, the onboard computer controls a virtual UAV in AirSim.

Project publication
